Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Still unsure? Here are the answers to the questions our clients ask most often before getting started.

🌐 How much does a website cost?
Pricing depends on your needs. A business website starts from 990€ and an e-commerce store from 1,990€. We also offer monthly plans from 490€/month including website, maintenance and social media support. View all plans →
⏱️ How long does it take to build my website?
We usually deliver a business website in 10 to 21 days and an e-commerce store in 3 to 4 weeks. More advanced projects such as apps or AI systems usually take 30 to 60 days. Everything starts with a free audit to define a precise timeline.
🎨 I know nothing about design. Do you handle everything?
Absolutely. You do not need technical knowledge. You share your ideas, activity and preferences, and we handle the rest: logo, visual identity, website, social media visuals and launch assets. You review, approve and move forward.
📱 Do you also create visuals for social media?
Yes. We create posts, stories, reels and templates for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. Depending on your plan, we can also manage your full social media presence, including publishing, comments and editorial strategy.
💳 How do payments work?
For custom projects: 50% at order, 50% at delivery. Monthly plans are billed monthly by card or bank transfer. We can also arrange staged payments for projects above 1,500€.
📊 How do I know if it works?
We define clear indicators from day one: traffic, conversion rate, generated leads, social engagement and operational savings. You receive regular reporting, and our clients typically see stronger ROI and lower operating costs within a few months.
🌍 Do you work with clients outside France?
Yes. Hozana Concept works remotely with clients across several markets. Our delivery model is cloud-based, so strategy sessions, design reviews, automation setup and reporting can all be managed online.
🚀 What is the first step?
Start with our free 30-minute audit. Fill out the form above and an expert will contact you within 24h. We analyze your situation, objectives and budget, then propose the most suitable solution with no commitment.
